WHO WE ARE

Creating stronger communities through partnership.

Partners That Care International brings people, organisations and communities together around a shared commitment to creating positive change.

We believe that lasting impact happens when people work together, share knowledge and create opportunities for others to thrive.

Our work connects organisations and individuals with the resources, relationships and opportunities they need to make a difference.

```
```
PTCI
OUR MISSION

Connecting people. Creating opportunities. Making an impact.

Our mission is to build meaningful connections between people and organisations so that communities can benefit from collaboration, support and shared resources.

We focus on practical partnerships that create opportunities, strengthen relationships and encourage positive community development.
